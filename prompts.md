# Prompts

**Style lock (prefix every prompt):** Live-action cinematic sci-fi, realistic actors, 35mm film, shallow depth of field, cold blue and amber light, mountain-pyramid city, no text.

5. Zei and Bai at screens showing a glowing grid of moving light patterns.
6. Zei's pattern collapses on screen; Mu disappointed, Fin frightened in the crowd.
7. Zei's face: sudden realisation, typing fast.
8. Audience cheers; Bai raises her hand and concedes.
9. Waiting room: Zei alone puts a dark purple letter in his pocket.
